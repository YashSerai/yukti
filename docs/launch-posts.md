## Product post

I built Yukti for the Agentic Commerce Hackathon.

It remembers who people are to you, finds a current gift, asks for exact approval, then opens a scoped Prava checkout. The core flow starts in iMessage and keeps the product source, budget, permission, and transaction state visible.

Try it: https://yukti.yashns.chatgpt.site

## Build post

One text teaches Yukti who Sarah is, what she likes, the budget I prefer, and where the gift needs to go. It turns that into editable memory, checks a live merchant page, and stops for approval before opening Prava's Visa checkout.

Built with Codex, Linq, Senso, Gemini, and Prava.

@OpenAIDevs @Visa @PravaPayments
